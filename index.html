About the Council Am y Cyngor

Llansannan Community Council is the first tier of local government serving the Bro Aled community in Conwy, north Wales. The council represents local residents, consults on planning applications, manages community assets, awards grants to local organisations and groups, and works with Conwy County Borough Council and other public bodies on matters affecting the area.

The council meets monthly (except August) at Canolfan Addysg Bro Aled, Llansannan at 7:30pm. All meetings are open to the public and press. A public participation period is available at the start of each meeting.

The council area covers the community of Llansannan and the parish of Bylchau, within the Conwy County Borough Council area.

Llansannan Community Council is committed to the Welsh Language and all communications are available in both Welsh and English · Mae Cyngor Cymuned Llansannan wedi ymrwymo i'r Gymraeg

Planning Cynllunio

Planning matters within the Llansannan area fall under Conwy County Borough Council. Llansannan Community Council is a statutory consultee on planning applications within its area and submits formal responses on behalf of local residents. Council responses are recorded in the monthly minutes.

The council is a statutory consultee but does not determine planning applications — decisions are made by Conwy County Borough Council · Nid y cyngor sy'n penderfynu ar geisiadau cynllunio — gwneir penderfyniadau gan Gyngor Bwrdeistref Sirol Conwy

Our Area — Where is Llansannan? Ein Hardal — Ble mae Llansannan?

Llansannan is a village and community in Conwy County Borough, north Wales, situated in the scenic Dyffryn Aled valley (Aled Valley) in the ancient district of Bro Aled. The community lies approximately 9 miles south of Abergele and 14 miles south-east of Conwy, in the foothills of the Mynydd Hiraethog uplands.

The Llansannan Community Council area covers the community of Llansannan and the parish of Bylchau, encompassing a wide rural area of upland and valley farmland, small hamlets, and the main village of Llansannan itself.

The area is rich in Welsh language, culture and heritage. Llansannan is closely associated with some of the greatest figures in Welsh literary history, including the renowned poet Tudur Aled and hymn-writer William Rees (Gwilym Hiraethog).
